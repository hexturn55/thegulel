## 1. How the major apps acquire users: paid vs organic, UA spend, ad networks, ROAS/payback

### Takeaway
Paid social UA drives the global leaders, and they spend very heavily. In 2025, ReelShort's marketing ran at about **55% of revenue**. India's Kuku FM spent **₹285 cr on ads against ₹242 cr of revenue** in FY25. Meta (Facebook + Instagram) is the single biggest channel. The 2026 trend runs away from pure paid UA: the leaders are adding near-zero-CAC distribution (telco bundles, franchises, owned platforms) because AI-content oversupply has roughly **doubled traffic costs**.

### Cited Findings

**Global market scale and leaders (label: 2025 data unless noted)**
- Q1 2025: ReelShort in-app revenue grew 31% QoQ to **US$130M**. DramaBox grew 29% to **US$120M**. Cumulative global in-app revenue by March 2025 was **US$490M** for ReelShort and **US$450M** for DramaBox. Global short-drama IAP in Q1 2025 was about **US$700M**, nearly 4x Q1 2024. A wave of H2-2024 entrants grew fast: DramaWave, NetShort, FlickReels. — [Sensor Tower, State of Short Drama Apps 2025](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-short-drama-apps-2025).   - Note: one Digiday-sourced summary called these figures "in-app **ad** revenues". Sensor Tower's own wording is in-app (IAP) revenue, so treat them as IAP. — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/in-graphic-detail-the-rise-of-micro-dramas-that-are-attracting-big-ad-dollars/)

**ROAS/payback**
- Practitioner guidance says short-drama operators target **payback inside 7–30 days** and scale on ROAS over that window. Hook quality is named as the main controllable lever. — [Flicknexs blog](https://blog.flicknexs.com/micro-drama-app-marketing-distribution/) *(vendor blog, low authority)*
- Mobile Dev Memo warns against lengthening payback windows to excuse weak performance. Extend them only once enough cohort data exists. — [Mobile Dev Memo](https://mobiledevmemo.com/the-danger-of-extending-marketing-payback-windows/)

### Inferences
- A new entrant should plan for CAC that exceeds early revenue. ReelShort, at US$1B scale, still spent about 55% of revenue on marketing in 2025. Indian audio and video peers spent more than 100% of revenue (see Q6).
- Meta should be the core paid channel. In the US it takes about a third of micro-drama spend (FB 25% + IG 8%). *General knowledge, not sourced this session:* TikTok is banned in India, so Instagram Reels, Facebook, YouTube Shorts and Google App Campaigns will carry Gulel's paid mix. Treat the US split only as a directional benchmark.
- Gulel is an AI-produced catalogue. It enters the segment where supply is exploding and traffic costs have doubled. Differentiation (Hindi-native stories, Indian genres) and fast kill/scale discipline matter more than volume.
- The telco-bundle lever (ReelShort–AIS) is the one proven near-zero-CAC channel. An Indian equivalent would be Jio, Airtel or Vi bundles, but no Indian micro-drama telco deal was found this session.

## 2. Ad creative patterns that win for micro-drama

### Takeaway
The winning ad shows the story, not the app. It opens on betrayal, revenge or a twist in the first 3 seconds, with punchy lines and fast cuts, and it prioritizes emotional clarity over plot complexity. Creative volume is extreme: about 1,900 to 5,800 creatives per app per period (see findings), and SocialPeta tracks 80M+ short-drama creatives. In 2026, advantage comes from how fast you test, iterate and localize, not raw volume.

### Cited Findings
- Short-drama ads show "the beginning of a story: betrayal, conflict, revenge, or a sharp plot twist" instead of an "install the app" message. Many look "cringeworthy... genuinely wild" in the Facebook Ads Library and spy tools, "yet they consistently drive conversions at scale". The format is described as hard to monitor with spy tools. — [Palai.media, "Short Drama Ads: The Creative Format Hard to Spy On"](https://palai.media/en/articles/iak-pratsiuie-format-short-drama)
- SocialPeta identifies **three dominant creative formulas**, all "optimized for the first three seconds to maximize completion rates and align with short-video platform algorithms".
  - Formula 3 is "**plot twists + hard-hitting lines + dynamic editing**".
  - Across all three, "**emotional clarity outperforms narrative complexity**".
  - Localized creative "reduces cultural friction" and improves retention and conversion.
  - Strong "**memory points**" increase shareability and organic spread beyond paid traffic.
  - Source: [Business of Apps / SocialPeta, "From scale to sustainability" (2026)](https://www.businessofapps.com/insights/from-scale-to-sustainability-how-short-drama-app-marketing-will-be-redefined-in-2026/); full report: [SocialPeta x AMO Pictures, Insight into Global Micro Drama App Marketing for 2026 (PDF)](https://sp2cdn-idea-global.zingfront.com/report-preview/2026/SocialPeta-x-Amo-Pictures-%7C-Insight-into-Global-Micro-Drama-App-Marketing-for-2026.pdf). Formulas 1 and 2 were not in the retrieved snippets.
- **Creative volume benchmarks:**
  - **Mintegral report (released around early 2026, covering 2025):**
    - Short-drama app downloads rose **95.5%**.
    - Active advertising apps rose **132%** and ad creatives rose **151%**.
    - Creative density was **1,887**, the highest of any sector studied.
    - There are "clear seasonal peaks... standardised and repeatable launch cycles". Heading into 2026, "creative scale is no longer a competitive advantage — it is a minimum requirement". The differentiator is how efficiently advertisers "test, iterate, and localise creatives".
    - Sources: [Marketech APAC](https://marketech-apac.com/short-drama-app-downloads-hits-95-5-as-active-advertisers-rise-132-globally-report/); [Medianews4u](https://www.medianews4u.com/short-drama-app-downloads-surge-95-5-as-emerging-markets-drive-global-growth-mintegral-report/)
    - *Conflict:* another summary attributes the 1,887 figure to SocialPeta and to "H1 2026". It is most likely the same Mintegral stat.
  - **2025 average:** about **5,770 de-duplicated ad creatives per month** per short-drama app, with leaders "well into the millions". *(Attribution uncertain: the search summary drew on [Business of Apps, The 2025 short drama report](https://www.businessofapps.com/insights/the-2025-short-drama-report/) and [Real Reel](https://medium.com/real-reel/global-vertical-short-drama-platforms-2025-1-4e137598b1f3).)*
  - **SocialPeta's short-drama module:** tracks **90K+ active micro-drama advertisers, 80M+ creatives, and 50K+ new creatives daily**. — [SocialPeta short-drama solution page](https://socialpeta.com/en/solutions/short-drama-marketing)
- **AI creative and fatigue:**
  - 36Kr reports "frequent flops" in AI short-drama ads. — [36Kr](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3935991930961027)
  - The same result cluster says AI dramas were about 95% of roughly 130,000 micro-drama works launched in Q1 2026 (China). *(Attribution uncertain.)*
  - General app-UGC research: creative fatigues in **2–3 weeks**, and AI creative burns out on a similar timeline. Viewers show "format fatigue", not "creator fatigue". "Authenticity-coded", visibly human creator content earns a premium as synthetic creative fatigue rises. — [Trends.vc](https://trends.vc/synthetic-ugc-ads-ai-actors-managed-agencies-creative-fatigue/) *(not micro-drama specific)*
- **Format and hook claims (low authority):**
  - Micro-drama apps "hook viewers in the first three seconds, end every episode on a cliffhanger".
  - Contrarian claim: "Episode-2 unlock rate correlates with the hook in the first minute of the previous episode — not the cliffhanger at the end."
  - Advice to build 9:16 masters, burned-in subtitles and per-episode hook clips.
  - Source: [Flicknexs](https://blog.flicknexs.com/build-micro-drama-app/) *(vendor blog; attribution from search summary)*

### Inferences
- **What Gulel can copy.** Cut each pilot into several 15–45s "story-open" ads: betrayal, humiliation, reveal or supernatural jump in the first 3 seconds. Add big Hindi burned-in captions, one "hard-hitting line", and a cliffhanger freeze with a "watch Ep 2" CTA. Then build Hinglish and regional-caption variants, since localization is named as a lever.
- Gulel cannot match the competitors' volume. It has 23 titles and the category average is thousands of creatives per app. Make volume modular instead: for example 3–5 hook openings × 2 end-cards × 2 caption styles per pilot, and refresh every 1–2 weeks against the 2–3-week fatigue benchmark.
- AI-look creative has reported fatigue and "flop" issues. Mix in human creator reaction/UGC formats: an Indian creator reacting to the twist, or a "storytime" narration over clips.

- No retrieved data on specific **ad lengths**, **sound strategy**, caption styles, or a quantified **UGC-reaction vs story-cut** performance comparison for micro-drama.
- No AppGrowing, BigSpy or Meta Ad Library observations specific to Indian micro-drama advertisers (Kuku TV, Story TV, QuickTV).
- No public creatives-per-week or refresh-cadence figure for a named micro-drama app.

## 3. Pilot / test-and-greenlight strategies

### Takeaway
The clearest documented practice comes from Holywater (My Drama). It uses **AI pilots to "get data very quickly" before committing to full production**, and feeds that from a books app that tests hundreds of titles a month. Public numeric greenlight thresholds (CTR, CPI, ep-2 continuation, pay rate) were **not found**. The practitioner advice converges on measuring hook rate, CTR, completion and unlock/pay rates, and killing or scaling on **day-3 data**.

### Cited Findings
- **Holywater (My Drama):**
  - "Embeds AI tools across its workflow from localization and dubbing to **pilot generation and data testing**." It creates AI pilots "to get data very quickly" before committing to full production.
  - My Drama has 55M+ users. Its sister app **MyPassion "tests hundreds of titles each month"**, and the best are adapted into micro-dramas.
  - Per Holywater's own data, **AI-generated series convert paying users at almost exactly the same rate as live-action**.
  - Sources: [The Drop](https://www.dropmedia.co.uk/holywater-and-the-new-era-of-vertical-drama/); [Google Cloud case study](https://cloud.google.com/customers/holywater); [Holywater blog](https://www.holywater.tech/blog/how-holywaters-ai-powered-personalization-keeps-users-hooked)
- **Pratilipi (India):**
  - Uses its IP base of **20M+ stories** to pick microdrama adaptations through its studio Double Tap Films, which supplies Story TV.
  - About **140 Hindi titles** on the slate, and a target of **500 shows**.
  - Sources: [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/media-and-entertainment/build-habits-before-charging-users-pratilipi-targets-500-shows-to-solve-microdrama-content-shortage-110985.htm); [Hollywood Reporter India](https://www.hollywoodreporterindia.com/features/insight/indias-microdrama-boom-how-data-desire-and-aritifical-intelligence-are-reshaping-entertainment); [Buzzincontent, Flipkart 30-title deal](https://www.buzzincontent.com/branded-content/flipkart-signs-30-title-microdrama-deal-with-pratilipis-double-tap-films-12510280)
- **Kuku TV** releases "**hundreds of microdramas each month**" and has 150,000+ episodes, an extreme volume-testing posture. — [Adgully](https://www.adgully.com/post/14130/77-actors-power-kuku-tvs-ms-dhoni-microdrama-campaign); [Medianews4u](https://www.medianews4u.com/77-actors-fuel-kuku-tv-dhoni-led-campaign-momentum-with-nationwide-creator-push/)
- **Suggested pre-production test package:** 5 titles, 3 poster/thumbnail directions, 3 trailers, 2 episode-one edits, and one paid-social or organic test plan. "The cheapest time to learn is before the full run is shot." — [Vertical Haus](https://verticalhaus.ai/blog/reelshort-style-micro-drama-production) / [Pippit](https://www.pippit.ai/blog/video-agent/micro-drama-app) *(vendor blogs; exact attribution from search summary)*
- **Metrics suggested by practitioners (low authority):**
  - Run about 30-second cliffhanger clips on TikTok and Meta to measure **hook rate (3-second views)** and **CTR** to a waitlist.
  - Track **D1/D3/D7 return rates**.
  - "Kill or scale based on day-three data" using a dashboard of **completion rate and revenue per episode**.
  - Fund a 3–5 episode arc first and scale only if completion and unlock rates beat benchmark.
  - Source: [Influencers-Time](https://www.influencers-time.com/vertical-micro-drama-series-the-cliffhanger-format-stealing/) *(content-farm style site; use only as directional)*
- **Market backdrop for greenlighting:** about 90% of new AI titles lose money ([36Kr](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3938564540759430)). In India, one live-action series costs **₹20–70 lakh** to produce ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/indias-microdrama-boom-is-here-but-can-the-unit-economics-work/)).

### Inferences
- Gulel's 22 two-episode pilots already mirror the Holywater model: cheap AI pilots to generate data before full production. A workable Gulel scorecard per pilot:
  - **Ad layer:** hook rate (3s view %), thumb-stop, CTR, cost per Ep-1 start.
  - **Product layer:** Ep-1 completion %, **Ep-1→Ep-2 continuation %**, D1/D3 return.
  - **Money layer:** coin-unlock or pay rate on the Ep-2+ paywall, rewarded-ad unlock rate, revenue per Ep-1 viewer.
  - Rank pilots against each other within a genre on equal budgets, and decide at about day 3–7.
- Because two episodes can't prove a 20-episode arc, add a "wait-list / notify me for Ep 3" CTA. Treat opt-ins per 1,000 Ep-2 completions as a demand signal.

### Gaps
- **No public numeric thresholds** (e.g., "greenlight if CTR > X% or ep-2 continuation > Y%") from ReelShort, DramaBox, Kuku, or any named platform.
- I could not retrieve whether ReelShort or Chinese platforms test with trailers or partial seasons before shooting. The Chinese practice of small-budget "test drama" placement was not sourced this session.

## 4. Organic social: YouTube, Facebook, Reels, full-episode dumps, character accounts

### Takeaway
The leaders treat YouTube as a free-content funnel and brand channel. ReelShort's main channel has about 15–17M+ subscribers, with daily clips, free episode collections and **full movies**, plus localized language channels. DramaBox runs "Watch Full Series" and "All Free Full Dramas" playlists. Both DramaBox and GoodShort build **SEO pages targeting "free full episodes on YouTube"** searches. In India, Kuku TV's biggest organic push was a coordinated **cast/creator social blitz**: 77 actors for #DhoniWatchesKukuTV.

## 5. Funnel: where ads send users; web-to-app; web payments

### Takeaway
ReelShort and peers sell on **web as well as the app stores**. Web billing descriptors such as "ReelShortW" appear on card statements. The April 2025 US *Epic v. Apple* ruling opened external web checkout links from iOS in the US, and Stripe-style web billing costs about 3% vs 15–30% store fees. I found no source confirming whether micro-drama ads deep-link to the exact episode or route through web landing pages.

### Cited Findings
- ReelShort is available on the web, the Apple App Store and Google Play, and sells subscriptions, VIP plans and coins. - Micro-drama apps "typically integrate Apple and Google Play In-App Purchases, **Stripe web billing**, virtual coin ledgers and rewarded video ad networks". — [Idea Usher](https://ideausher.com/blog/reelshort-clone-app-development-cost/) *(dev-agency blog)*

It must be an external browser; an in-app WebView "is unlikely to pass App Store review". — [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/engineering/can-you-use-stripe-for-in-app-purchases); [TechCrunch, 1 May 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/01/stripe-shows-ios-developers-how-to-avoid-apples-app-store-commission/)

### Inferences
- Gulel's web plus iOS/Android footprint suits a **web-first paid funnel** for India:
  - Meta or YouTube ad → thegulel.com/[title]/ep1, playing instantly with no install.
  - Ep-2 paywall with a UPI/Razorpay coin pack (₹149), avoiding store fees.
  - "Get the app" prompt after first purchase, using a deferred deep link so the app opens on the same episode.
- This also gives clean pilot-level measurement, because web events are attributable per title. Run an app-install campaign in parallel as a control.

India's micro-drama market is big in users but thin in paying users. Lumikai counts about **US$300M revenue, 100M MAU and 17M payers in 2025**, with 450M+ downloads across 20+ apps, and says **no major platform is profitable**. ### Inferences
- **Spend reality for Gulel.** Well-funded Indian incumbents spend about ₹0.94–1.18 of advertising per ₹1 of revenue (Seekho, Kuku FM, FY25). Gulel cannot out-spend them. Its paid budget should be a **learning budget** (pilot selection), not a scale budget, until Ep-2 continuation and pay rates are proven.
- **Pricing and paywall context.**
  - Kuku charges about ₹899/yr and Story TV ₹1,099/yr.
  - Tadka and Fatafat are free and ad-supported; Tadka reached 100M users in about 2 months.
  - Pratilipi warns against monetizing too early.
  - This supports Gulel's rewarded-ad unlocks, and possibly more free episodes on the flagship title to build habit, before pushing coin packs.
- **Tactics Gulel can copy cheaply:**
  - (1) A cast/creator blitz, adapted for AI: pay 30–70 Hindi micro-creators to post reaction or duet videos on the same day for one pilot (a scaled-down #DhoniWatchesKukuTV).
  - (2) Cricket-moment and festival-timed creative, e.g. horror around specific calendar moments. Kuku timed campaigns to the IPL.
  - (3) Owned or partner cross-promotion, like QuickTV via ShareChat/Moj: seek a distribution partner (telco, OEM, or a content or social app) rather than relying only on Meta.
  - (4) Position the Hindi-first *original* slate against dubbed-Chinese catalogues.
- **AI positioning.** QuickTV (₹100 cr AI plan), Bullet ("AI-powered") and Holywater's data (AI converts like live-action) show AI production is now mainstream in India. Gulel's AI origin is not a differentiator by itself; story and genre fit must carry it.
