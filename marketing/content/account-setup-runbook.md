# Account setup runbook: every field, in order

Creating the accounts needs your phone/email verification codes and your acceptance of each platform's terms,
so a person has to do it. Everything to paste and upload is below. Budget about 90 minutes for all five accounts.
Bios and voices come from `02-channels-and-characters.md`; every image path is under `marketing/assets/`.

**Before you start**
- Use a work email/phone the company controls (e.g. `social@thegulel.com`), not a personal one. Store the logins in a password manager.
- Turn on **two-factor authentication** on every account as soon as it exists, and add a second admin.
- **Pace it.** Create the brand account first, then no more than two character accounts a day from the same phone.
  Bursts of new accounts from one device often trigger verification checks.
  Post 3–6 times on each new account before linking it anywhere or promoting it.
- If a handle is taken, use the fallback listed and send me the final handles. I'll update the site links, the launch
  graphics and the calendar, then re-render.

## Step 1: Meta Business Portfolio (the container for everything Meta)

1. Go to **business.facebook.com** → *Create a business portfolio*.
   - Business name: **Gulel Entertainment**
   - Your name, work email.
2. *Settings → Business info*: add the address, website `https://thegulel.com` and GST. Then *Security center → Start verification*.
   Verification takes a few days, so start now.
3. *Settings → Brand safety → Domains → Add* `thegulel.com`. Add the DNS TXT record it gives you in your domain host, then *Verify*.
4. Add a second admin under *Users → People*.
