**Why these four first.**
- **Pishachini** is the only full 20-episode series, so every follower can binge.
- The Indian micro-drama audience is **>70% male, 80% aged 18–34 and 60–75% Tier II/III**. Revenge and
  hidden-identity aspiration tropes are what that audience responds to, so **वारिस** (the servant's son who comes back rich)
  and the **अंडरकवर CEO** are launch accounts, not afterthoughts.
- The **Bride** covers the romance/confession audience and the diary format, the cheapest content to make daily.
- **All four come from series that are clearly real productions.** Eight live series carry a `__demo__` tag
  (`data/live-catalog.md`). None of them gets a character account or ad spend until someone has watched its Episode 1.
- **Wave 2** characters launch only for series that pass the pilot tournament (`04-paid-media.md` §4).
  An account for a show we won't make is wasted effort.

**Ownership.** Everything sits in one Meta Business Portfolio and one Google account with Brand Accounts
(`05-tracking-and-funnel.md` §5–6). Two admins per account, 2FA on. Every character account is a
**Creator/Business professional account** with Instagram's **"AI-generated profile"** label switched on.

## 3. Platform rules that shape everything

- **Instagram / Facebook / Threads:** tick "AI info" on every photoreal clip. Character accounts carry the account-level
  **"AI-generated profile"** label; since 31 Aug 2026, undisclosed AI-person profiles lose Reels/Explore reach.
  Use **Collab** instead of cross-posting, **Trial Reels** for hook tests, and **Broadcast channels** for drop alerts.
- **YouTube:** tick **"Altered or synthetic content"** at upload. Since May 2026 Shorts show an on-video AI label, which
  doesn't reduce reach. Links in Shorts descriptions aren't clickable, so use **Related video** → full pilot.
  Use Test & Compare on long-form thumbnails.
- **ASCI:** a character account is a "virtual influencer" and must say upfront it isn't a real human. Keep it in the bio
  *and* on the graphics. Any brand-integration post needs a second, paid-partnership disclosure.
- **Likeness:** AI faces must not resemble real actors or public figures. Check each face against a reverse-image search before launch.

## 4. AI character bibles

**Canon rule.** None of the 23 live synopses names a character (the only name in the data is *Raghu*, from Pishachini's
episode titles). So every account uses a **role name** (पिशाचिनी, वारिस, अंडरकवर CEO, बदली हुई दुल्हन) and only states
what the synopsis states. Everything marked *invented* below is new in-world colour, free to use as long as it
doesn't contradict the episodes. **Watch Episode 1 of each show before the account's first post**, and if a
character is named on screen, rename the display name to match.

**Every account, every time**
- Instagram **"AI-generated profile"** label on. Bio says *AI character*. Every graphic carries the on-image AI chip.
- Collab with `@thegulel` for any episode clip. Never re-post the brand's identical clip.
- Replies in character. If someone sincerely asks for medical, legal or financial help, drop the persona and point them to real help.
- One CTA per post: Episode 1 → `thegulel.com/go/{alias}` (link in bio / Story link sticker).
