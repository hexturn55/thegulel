# 01 · Gulel launch marketing master plan (Q4 2026)

**Owner:** Gulel marketing · **Window:** 28 Sep – 27 Dec 2026 (12 weeks + week 0) · **Budget:** ₹2.4–2.9 Cr for the
12 weeks, inside the ₹15 Cr Year-1 marketing line of the investor deck.

## 1. The plan in one paragraph

Gulel has something no Indian competitor has: **23 AI-produced series live at once**. *Pishachini: The Healer*
is a complete 20-episode horror series, and 22 more are two-episode pilots, each made for a few thousand rupees. So the launch
is also a **greenlight engine**. We blast every pilot across a Gulel brand channel and four in-world **AI character accounts**,
push paid traffic straight into the free Episode 1 player, and measure each pilot from hook to paywall to purchase.
Within three weeks the audience has told us which **3–4 shows deserve full 52-episode seasons**. Those shows get the budget,
the creators and the Diwali push. The character accounts turn one-off viewers into followers who come back for every drop,
and that organic reach is how the deck's **100M-viewer** goal becomes reachable on a **₹15 Cr** budget.

## 3. Strategy: five engines

1. **Pilot tournament (paid).** 22 pilots + the Pishachini control, 3–5 hook cuts each, ranked against each other on equal budgets
   (₹1,500–3,500/day × 7 days; ₹2.2–5.5 L for the whole slate). Kill on day 3, greenlight on day 7 (`04-paid-media.md` §4). Winners get Advantage+ scale, Demand Gen Shorts and
   creators. Losers stay live organically.
2. **Character flywheel (organic).** Four AI character accounts post in-world content daily, answer comments in character,
   and co-post with the brand as **Collabs**. They are clearly labelled AI, as the law and Instagram both require.
   Followers of a character are the cheapest re-engagement audience we will ever have.
3. **Free-episode funnel (conversion).** Every link goes to `thegulel.com/go/{show}` → the Episode 1 player (no login) → the Ep 2
   paywall → OTP sign-up → coins (UPI), rewarded ad or VIP. Tracked end to end with Pixel + CAPI + GA4 (`05-tracking-and-funnel.md`).
4. **YouTube as the free top of the funnel.** Free Episode 1 uploads plus Shorts cuts linked by "Related video". After the tournament,
   killed pilots go up in full to harvest subscribers. ReelShort built a 15M+-subscriber channel on free episodes.
5. **Moments.** Front-load testing before festive CPMs rise 30–50% (Navratri 11 Oct → Diwali 8 Nov).
   Then use Diwali's amavasya night for *Pishachini* (#AmavasyaKiRaat), coin bonuses on Dhanteras–Diwali (6–8 Nov),
   a UP/Bihar push around Chhath (13–16 Nov) and a New-Year binge offer.

## 4. Audience (research)

- **Who:** >70% male, 80% aged 18–34, 60–75% Tier II/III; ad targeting starts at 18 (DPDP). Deck footprint: 15 states.
- **How they find shows:** 89% first saw a micro-drama on Instagram/Facebook; only 5% through friends.
- **When:** evenings (69%); lunch and commute breaks are secondary. Post and schedule for **19:00–23:30 IST**.
- **What hooks them:** revenge, hidden identity, sudden wealth, betrayal, village and folk horror.
  Devanagari for Tier 2/3, Hinglish for metros.
- **What they pay:** India micro-drama earns about ₹1,300 per payer per year. Peers sell ₹1–₹2 trials rolling into UPI AutoPay
  (Kuku TV: ₹399/quarter, ₹899/year), so Gulel's ₹149 entry pack and ₹399/week VIP are priced high (§8).

## 8. Product changes that make the marketing work (recommended, not in this PR)

| Change | Why | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Decide **1 vs 5 free episodes** and make ads match | The deck says 5, the live pilots give 1. Ads must promise what the product does | Google Dishonest Pricing policy; Meta misleading-claims policy |
| Add a **₹19–₹49 sachet** (20–40 coins) and a **₹1–₹2 VIP first-week trial** on UPI AutoPay | ₹149 is a big first step for Tier 2/3. Peers convert on ₹1–₹2 trials; 70–80% of micro-drama subscribers authorise mandates | India benchmarks research |
| Reprice **VIP weekly ₹399 → ₹99** (test ₹199/month) | Kuku TV charges ₹399 per *quarter* | India benchmarks research |
| **UPI for VIP**: move web VIP checkout from card-only Stripe (USD default) to Razorpay subscriptions / UPI AutoPay | 70–80% of Indian micro-drama subscribers pay by UPI AutoPay | Research report; `src/app/api/subscriptions/checkout/route.ts` |
| **App links**: serve `/.well-known/apple-app-site-association` + `assetlinks.json` and add associated domains to `mobile/app.json` | Ad and bio links can't open the installed app today; needed for Google Web-to-App | Funnel research |
| **One Google tag path**: use either `NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_ID` (gtag) or a GA4 tag inside GTM, not both | Both are wired in `src/components/Analytics.tsx`; setting both double-counts | Research report |
| **Phone OTP first** inside Instagram/Facebook browsers | Google login fails there (`disallowed_useragent`) | Funnel research; this PR adds the in-app-browser hint |
| **Android app live** on Play with per-series custom store listings | Unlocks Google App campaigns (the cheapest installs in India: ₹10–45 CPI) | Google toolchain research |
| **DPDP consent** notice + marketing opt-ins | Consent Manager framework from 13 Nov 2026; full duties 13 May 2027 | Funnel research |

## 9. KPIs and cadence

- **Daily (15 min):** spend, cost per Ep-1 viewer, CTR by ad, top comments per account.
- **Monday review (45 min):** the per-series scoreboard (`05-tracking-and-funnel.md` §7). Kill, keep or greenlight each pilot.
  Pick the top 3 hooks to re-cut for other series.
- **Weekly report to founders:** viewers (paid/organic), sign-ups, payers, revenue, D7 ROAS, follower growth per account,
  greenlight decisions. The investor dashboard (`/investors/dashboard`) is the money source of truth.

## 11. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| AI backlash ("AI slop") or reach penalties | Label everything. Make AI the story ("the first AI-native drama studio"). Show BTS craft. Never deny AI |
| Undisclosed AI profile → Instagram demotion | "AI-generated profile" label on day 1; bio disclosure; on-graphic chip |
| Ad rejections (sexual, violent, shocking hooks) | Evoke, don't show. Gore-free cuts. Pre-approve 3 safe hooks per series |
| Paid CAC outruns ARPPU (Kuku FM spent 118% of revenue on ads) | Kill rules at day 3. Cheaper entry price (§8). Organic character engine carries reach |
| Duplicate-content suppression across our own accounts | Collab posts; character accounts only post transformed edits |
| Festive CPM inflation | Test before 11 Oct. Spend festive money on offers, not cold tests |
| Look-alike faces of real people | Reverse-image check each character face; YouTube likeness detection gives a fast takedown path |
