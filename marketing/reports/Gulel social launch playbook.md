# Gulel Should Buy Signal Before Scale

Gulel should launch as a web-first "pilot tournament". The brand accounts give away every pilot's free episode. A small cast of clearly labelled AI-character accounts posts in-world material that adds something new rather than reposting the same clips. Equal-budget Meta Reels and YouTube Shorts ads send Hindi-belt viewers to an Episode-1 web player, and its Episode-2 paywall ranks the 22 pilots on continuation and pay rate within about a week. That design suits India, where **89% of micro-drama viewers first meet the format in Instagram and Facebook feeds** ([Meta × Ormax](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/social-feeds-are-powering-indias-micro-drama-boom-meta-ormax-report/)), and where the incumbent Kuku FM spent **₹285 Cr on advertising against ₹242 Cr of revenue in FY25** ([Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/fintrackr/kuku-fm-reports-rs-240-cr-revenue-in-fy25-spends-rs-285-cr-on-marketing-10943137)). Gulel cannot run this design yet. The live site sends Meta only `PageView` and `share` events. It has no Conversions API, no UTM capture and no Universal or App Links, and its web VIP checkout is card-only Stripe ([checkout route](/home/user/thegulel/src/app/api/subscriptions/checkout/route.ts)), in a market where **70–80% of micro-drama subscribers pay by UPI AutoPay** ([FICCI-EY via Medianews4u](https://www.medianews4u.com/indias-microdrama-market-set-to-grow-50-annually-driven-by-tier-ii-iii-adoption-ai-production-and-upi-autopay-payments-ficci-ey-report/)). Every Gulel frame is "synthetically generated information" under India's IT Rules amendment, in force since 20 Feb 2026. That makes AI labels on Instagram, YouTube, Google Ads and ASCI-covered posts a condition of reach, not a choice. The investor deck overreaches. At India's Android install costs, ₹15 Cr of Year-1 marketing buys roughly **3–15 million installs**, so "100 million viewers by 2027" holds only if "viewer" means social reach. The ₹399/week VIP costs as much as Kuku TV charges for a full quarter. The deck's "5 free episodes" contradicts the live one-free setting that every ad has to describe. The next three weeks, before Navratri lifts ad prices 30–50%, are best spent fixing measurement, adding a UPI trial as the first purchase, and running the pilot tournament.

## Incumbents win on story-first ads, free episodes and heavy spend

The global leaders are paid-social machines that are only now learning to spend less. ReelShort's in-app revenue grew to **US$130M in Q1 2025** and DramaBox's to **US$120M** ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-short-drama-apps-2025)). A Media Partners Asia report projects **US$1.05B of ReelShort revenue and its first profit in 2026**. In that projection, marketing falls from **55% of revenue in 2025 to 44% by 2028**, and the savings come from franchises and telco bundles, such as Thailand's AIS, that "add subscribers at almost no acquisition cost" ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/reelshort-1-billion-revenue-profit-2026-mpa-1236828885/); [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/08/microdrama-reelshort-revenue-profit-1237027492/)). ReelShort's move to bill through its own web store instead of paying app-store commissions of up to 30% is credited as a profit driver ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/323399/20260806/reelshort-hits-1-billion-microdrama-revenue-first-profit-driven-app-store-bypass.htm)). In late August its web store charged **US$11.99 for the first week, then US$14.99 a week, against US$19.99 on iOS** ([Filmustage](https://filmustage.com/blog/short-drama-apps-compared-reelshort-vs-dramabox-in-2026/)). In the US, **68% of micro-drama ad spend went to social media** in Jan–Sep 2025: Facebook 25%, TikTok 19%, Snapchat 16%, Instagram 8% and YouTube only 2% ([Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/in-graphic-detail-the-rise-of-micro-dramas-that-are-attracting-big-ad-dollars/)). The cost trend is the warning for an AI-native entrant. Overseas micro-drama ad spend passed **US$20M a day in mid-2026, about 70% of it on AI-generated dramas**, while traffic costs rose more than 100% year on year ([36Kr/DataEye](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3919271564154503); [36Kr](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3858360239799168)). Roughly **90% of new AI titles lose money** ([36Kr](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3938564540759430)).

The creative that wins shows the story, not the app. The ads open on "betrayal, conflict, revenge, or a sharp plot twist", and they convert at scale even when they look "cringeworthy" ([Palai.media](https://palai.media/en/articles/iak-pratsiuie-format-short-drama)). SocialPeta's formulas are all built for the first three seconds, and one is simply "plot twists + hard-hitting lines + dynamic editing". Its research also finds that **emotional clarity outperforms narrative complexity** and that localised creative lifts conversion ([Business of Apps / SocialPeta](https://www.businessofapps.com/insights/from-scale-to-sustainability-how-short-drama-app-marketing-will-be-redefined-in-2026/)). Creative density in the category, at **1,887**, is the highest of any sector Mintegral measured, and "creative scale is no longer a competitive advantage — it is a minimum requirement" ([Marketech APAC](https://marketech-apac.com/short-drama-app-downloads-hits-95-5-as-active-advertisers-rise-132-globally-report/)). Creative fatigues in two to three weeks, and visibly human creator content earns a premium as synthetic ads saturate feeds ([Trends.vc](https://trends.vc/synthetic-ugc-ads-ai-actors-managed-agencies-creative-fatigue/)). Pocket FM's CEO says lifting CTR from **2% to 2.25% cut acquisition cost by about 30%** ([Rohan Nayak on X](https://x.com/RohanNayak2/status/2098104784264065525)). That is the clearest statement that hooks, not bids, drive CAC.

Organic distribution is mainly free-episode giveaways. ReelShort's YouTube channel has **15–17M+ subscribers** and posts daily clips, "free movie episode collections" and **full movies**, plus a cast behind-the-scenes series. It also runs a "Fandom" hub on its own site to capture "how to watch ReelShort for free" searches ([ReelShort Fandom](https://www.reelshort.com/fandom/how-to-watch-reelshort-for-free-01-25-1a2b555/)). DramaBox runs "All Free Full Dramas" playlists ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLdJBGEOt8PovxBvcCHoLBHPp4CFjz_l71)) and SEO tag pages for "free full episodes youtube" ([DramaBox](https://www.dramabox.com/tag/87520)). GoodShort even targets the competitor's keyword with a "dramabox free episodes on youtube" page ([GoodShort](https://www.goodshort.com/tag/dramabox-free-episodes-on-youtube-playlets-videos)).

India follows the same pattern with more cricket and more free rivals. Lumikai counts **US$300M+ of 2025 revenue, 100M MAU and 17M payers across 450M+ downloads**, and a filmmaker quoted in the coverage sees no major platform operating profitably ([BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/trending/entertainment/story/can-india-monetise-microdramas-as-the-market-booms-but-profitability-remains-elusive-539568-2026-06-28)). The peers spend roughly a rupee on ads per rupee of revenue: Kuku FM's ratio is 1.18 and Seekho spent **₹134 Cr on advertising for ₹142 Cr of revenue** ([Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/fintrackr/seekho-spends-rs-134-cr-on-advertising-for-rs-142-cr-revenue-in-fy25-11205187)). Kuku adds MS Dhoni as investor and ambassador ([YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/03/ms-dhoni-joins-kuku-as-investor-brand-ambassador-of-microdrama-app)). Its **#DhoniWatchesKukuTV** blitz had **77 of its actors posting on the same day**, and it releases "hundreds of microdramas each month" ([Adgully](https://www.adgully.com/post/14130/77-actors-power-kuku-tvs-ms-dhoni-microdrama-campaign)). QuickTV passed **10M downloads in three months** by cross-posting to its sister apps Moj and ShareChat, and plans up to **₹100 Cr for AI micro-drama production** ([Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/technology/inside-sharechats-financial-turnaround-the-next-big-bet-on-microdrama)). ZEE's Bullet passed 10M Play downloads with gamified viewing ([Adgully](https://www.adgully.com/post/14420/bullet-microdrama-app-crosses-10-million-downloads-on-google-playstore)). The most important competitive fact for a paywalled app is that the giants are giving the format away. Amazon's MX Fatafat launched free with Munawar Faruqui ([Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/03/amazon-mx-player-fatafat-microdrama-service-india-1236762793/)). **JioHotstar Tadka reached 100M users in about two months**, free and ad-supported, after launching less than a week into IPL 2026 ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/tadka-jiohotstar-100-million-users-microdrama-1236771461/)). Pratilipi, which supplies Story TV, warns that "80% of microdrama apps will fail trying to monetise too soon" ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/media-and-entertainment/build-habits-before-charging-users-pratilipi-targets-500-shows-to-solve-microdrama-content-shortage-110985.htm)).

The lesson for Gulel is where to compete, not how much to spend. Kuku FM's FY25 ad spend is **19 times Gulel's whole Year-1 marketing budget**. AI production is not a differentiator either: QuickTV, Bullet and Kuku all advertise it, and Holywater's data shows AI series convert payers at almost the same rate as live action ([The Drop](https://www.dropmedia.co.uk/holywater-and-the-new-era-of-vertical-drama/)). Gulel has four levers that cost little. The first is Hindi-native story hooks, set against the dubbed catalogues. The second is free-episode distribution on YouTube. The third is a creator blitz scaled down from Kuku's cast blitz. The fourth is faster kill-and-scale decisions than anyone running live-action shoots can manage.

The numbers point to cheap reach, expensive payers and a thin margin between them. Dividing the ₹10–45 CPI by the 3.8% download-to-payer ratio gives a **cost per first payer of roughly ₹260–1,180**. The Lumikai payer count includes ₹1–2 trial subscribers, so the real figure is probably higher. Against that, a payer who buys only Gulel's ₹149 starter pack never pays back. The model works only if payers repeat towards the category's ~₹1,300 yearly ARPPU. Kuku FM ran at about **₹300 CAC against ₹600 ARPU** in FY23 ([GrowthX](https://growthx.club/blog/kukufm-business-model)), a 2:1 ratio Gulel should treat as its first profitability target. Kuku and STAGE both lead with a ₹1–2 trial that rolls into an auto-renewing plan, and STAGE called auto-payments its "booster shot" ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/startups/how-stage-is-shaking-up-netflix-amazon-with-its-regional-originals/)). Pocket FM gives **up to 30 free minutes of unlocks per show per day** ([Pocket FM](https://pocketfm.com/support/pricing-and-coins)). Both patterns build habit before asking for money, which is what Pratilipi advises. The payment details matter as well. The new UPI merchant fee from 15 Oct 2026 applies only to transactions above ₹2,000, and AutoPay mandates are exempt ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/finance/news/mdr-on-upi-payments-what-customers-merchants-need-to-know-from-oct-15-126092401287_1.html); [BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/investment/story/upi-mdr-from-october-15-what-happens-to-auto-debit-payments-for-mutual-funds-insurance-and-ott-subscriptions-555732-2026-09-15)). Only Gulel's ₹2,499 Mega pack and a one-shot yearly VIP would pay it. More than **20M UPI mandates are cancelled every month, mostly for insufficient balance** ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/upi-discretionary-spending-bars-restaurants-autopay-revocations-august-2025-125090800646_1.html)), so VIP renewals should fall early in the month, with retry logic.

The audience is young, male, small-town and evening-heavy. More than **70% of India's micro-drama audience is male** and **60–75% comes from Tier II/III cities** ([FICCI-EY](https://www.medianews4u.com/indias-microdrama-market-set-to-grow-50-annually-driven-by-tier-ii-iii-adoption-ai-production-and-upi-autopay-payments-ficci-ey-report/)). About 80% are aged 18–34 ([The Established](https://www.theestablished.com/culture/entertainment/microdramas-in-india-11775974)). **69% watch in the evening to relax** ([afaqs, Google × Qualtrics](https://www.afaqs.com/news/media/google-youtube-eye-the-growing-260-mn-indian-micro-drama-pie-10918399)), and half prefer native-language content ([Campaign Asia](https://www.campaignasia.com/article/microdramas-test-indias-appetite-for-stories-in-120-seconds/atgs3blyqjs0qv6pz2qgrv4ayx)). FICCI-EY lists the tropes that land in Tier II as "affluent lifestyles, unexpected windfalls, concealed identities and rapid success arcs". Those map directly onto CEO-billionaire, substitute-bride and revenge pilots. Horror is not on the list but fits the male evening viewer.

For copy, the only guidance available is vendor guidance. It favours Devanagari Hindi for Tier 2/3 and older viewers and Roman-script Hinglish for metros and 18–34s ([Lapis](https://www.trylapis.com/resources/indian-language-ai-ads-guide)). An older analysis warns that Hinglish underperforms outside the Hindi states ([Quartz](https://qz.com/india/1767016/indian-brands-must-move-from-hinglish-to-tamil-telugu-malayalam)). Gulel should therefore treat its first two weeks as a language test cell with three arms: Devanagari hook text for UP, Bihar and Rajasthan ad sets, Hinglish for Delhi, Maharashtra and Gujarat, and English as a control. The voiceover stays Hindi throughout. The CTA must match the live product. "पहला एपिसोड फ़्री — आगे क्या हुआ?" or "Pehla episode free. Aage kya hua?" is accurate. "Episode 2 free" and "watch free" are not.

## Twenty-two pilots can be ranked for under ₹6 lakh

Gulel's slate already mirrors the best-documented test-and-greenlight model in the category. Holywater makes AI pilots "to get data very quickly" before it commits to full production, feeding them from a sister app that "tests hundreds of titles each month" ([The Drop](https://www.dropmedia.co.uk/holywater-and-the-new-era-of-vertical-drama/); [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/customers/holywater)). No platform publishes numeric greenlight thresholds. Practitioners converge on hook rate, CTR, completion, D1/D3/D7 return and unlock rate, and advise killing or scaling on **day-three data** ([Influencers-Time](https://www.influencers-time.com/vertical-micro-drama-series-the-cliffhanger-format-stealing/), low authority). With no external benchmark, Gulel should rank pilots **against each other**, within genre, on equal budgets, in the same week and with the same audience.

The tournament should be cheap by design. Each pilot gets its own ad-budget-optimised (ABO) ad set in one Meta campaign, targeting Hindi-belt users aged 18+. Each ad set runs three hook variants cut from the same Episode 1 at ₹1,500–3,500 a day for seven days, optimising for landing-page views or `ViewContent`, because no pilot will reach 50 purchases a week. Take a traffic CPM of ₹35–60 and assume Pocket FM's 2% CTR. A click then costs roughly **₹1.75–3**, so ₹10,000–25,000 per pilot buys about 3,000–14,000 landing clicks. That is enough to read Episode-1 completion and Episode-2 continuation, though not purchases. The whole slate costs **₹2.2–5.5 lakh, under 0.4% of the Year-1 marketing budget**. A mirrored YouTube Shorts test on Demand Gen, set to the Shorts-only channel, adds a second read at similar cost. Instagram Trial Reels, which show a Reel to non-followers first ([Instagram](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)), add a free organic read.

| Layer | Metric | Read from |
|---|---|---|
| Ad | Hook rate (3-s plays ÷ impressions), hold rate, link CTR, cost per Ep-1 start | Meta custom columns; Google Ads |
| Product | Ep-1 completion, **Ep-1 → Ep-2 continuation**, D1/D3 return | GA4 funnel on `video_start` / `video_complete` / `paywall_view` |
| Money | Ep-2 unlock by any method, paid unlock rate, rewarded-ad unlock rate, revenue per Ep-1 viewer | Server ledger, joined to first-touch UTM |
| Demand | "Notify me for Ep 3" opt-ins per 1,000 Ep-2 completions | First-party table |

A pricing confound will distort the money layer, so design around it. A pilot's Episode 2 costs 10 coins, about ₹15, but the smallest purchase is the ₹149 starter pack. A viewer has to spend ten times the episode's price to see a two-episode story end. The pay rate therefore measures tolerance for the ₹149 ticket as much as appetite for the story. Rewarded ads grant 5 coins, so two ads unlock an episode. Gulel should rank pilots mainly on **unlock by any method** plus notify-me opt-ins, and treat paid unlock as a tiebreaker until a smaller first purchase exists. A two-episode pilot cannot prove a 20- or 52-episode arc. The notify-me list is the cheapest proxy for season demand.

Sequencing the YouTube giveaway matters too. ReelShort-style full uploads are the right long-run play, but uploading a full two-episode pilot free on YouTube during test week would let viewers bypass the web paywall and contaminate the signal. During the tournament, post only Episode 1 and cliffhanger Shorts. After the read-out, upload killed pilots in full to harvest watch time and subscribers. Upload the winners' pilots in full as the free on-ramp to paid Episode 3 onward. A starting greenlight rule, to be calibrated rather than trusted, is this: Episode-1 completion and paywall-to-sign-up at or above the slate median, and D0 revenue at least 70% of the best pilot's on matched spend. Greenlighting is cheap. A full 52-episode season costs **₹37,000–1.76 lakh** on the deck's own two production paths ([investor-deck-numbers.md](/home/user/thegulel/marketing/data/investor-deck-numbers.md)), less than half a day of spend at the planned ₹15 Cr pace. So Gulel should greenlight two to four winners within days and put the creator and character budget behind them. Spreading that budget across all 22 pilots would waste it.

## Brand accounts carry the episodes, and character accounts must add something new

### Platform playbooks now punish duplication

All three major platforms have turned against reposting, and this rule shapes the multi-account plan more than any growth tactic does. Instagram replaces duplicate Reels with the "original" in recommendations ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-algorithm-overhaul-will-reward-original-content-and-penalize-aggregators-130018977.html)). Since April 2026 it also stops recommending to non-followers any account that "primarily share[s] content they didn't create, or haven't meaningfully transformed", with Collab co-authoring and native Repost as the sanctioned ways to share ([PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/)). Facebook cuts distribution and monetisation for unoriginal reposts ([Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/07/223-meta-cracks-down-on-unoriginal-facebook-content/)). YouTube's July 2025 "inauthentic content" policy covers templated, mass-produced videos with minimal variation ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-clarifies-monetization-update-inauthentic-repeated-content/752892/)). The risk for Gulel is not stealing other people's clips. It is **looking like a template farm, or posting identical clips across its own accounts**.

On Instagram, the brand account @gulel posts each episode clip once, as a Collab with the relevant character account, so one original post earns reach on both grids. New hooks go out as Trial Reels first, and winners move to the grid and into paid ads by Post ID, which keeps their likes and comments. The bio carries five links ([inro.social](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-affiliate-links)). Story link stickers are open to every account. Feed captions are not clickable, so every feed CTA points to the bio. Meta Business Suite schedules up to 25 posts a day, 30 days ahead, across linked accounts ([SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/how-to-schedule-instagram-posts/)). That is enough for the deck's "daily drops" promise. On YouTube, one Google account can run **up to 100 channels as Brand Accounts**, but a severe violation on one can hit every channel on the same AdSense identity ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7001996?hl=en)). That argues for keeping compilation-style uploads on the brand channel. Links in Shorts descriptions have not been clickable since 2023 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/10/youtube-is-disabling-links-on-shorts-to-cut-down-on-spam)). Every Short should therefore use the **Related video** slot to point at the full Episode-1 or full-pilot upload ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en)), whose description carries a clickable thegulel.com link. Test & Compare runs up to three title or thumbnail variants and picks the winner on **watch time** ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-expands-a-b-testing-to-include-titles-alongside-thumbnails/)), which suits Episode-1 uploads. Auto-dubbing, open to all creators since February 2026, covers 27 languages with expressive speech in Hindi ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-expands-auto-dubbing-to-all-creators/811375/)). That gives Gulel a nearly free test of demand in English, Spanish, Portuguese and Indonesian.

### AI characters work as TV-character accounts, not virtual influencers

India will follow openly artificial personas at scale. FUTR Studios' Kyra launched in January 2022 and passed **250,000 Instagram followers by August 2024**, with a reported (unaudited) engagement rate of about 10% and deals with Amazon Prime Video and boAt ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyra_%28virtual_influencer%29); [virtualhumans.org](https://www.virtualhumans.org/article/kyra-indias-first-virtual-influencer-has-over-100-000-followers)). Both are lifestyle personas selling brands. Gulel's characters sell a story. They should borrow Kyra's and Naina's formats: trend audio, "day in my life" and look reveals, all performed in character. Their CTA should always be "see what happened in Episode 1". No sourced case study of a fiction-series character account was found, so Gulel will be building this format rather than copying it.

The cast should start small and follow the winners. A standard Meta Business Portfolio reportedly caps at **25 Instagram accounts**, each linked to a Facebook Page ([Sendwin](https://blog.send.win/meta-business-suite-manage-multiple-instagram-accounts-limit-multi-account-management-guide-2026/), single source). Each account also needs a steady stream of distinct material to escape the originality penalties. So launch with the brand account, Pishachini herself, and two or three characters from the pilots the team rates strongest. Add an account only when a pilot wins the tournament, and archive the accounts of killed pilots rather than leaving them dormant. Characters should post POV confessions, diary entries, in-character reactions to fan theories, and "what happens next" Story polls whose results feed alternate-ending Shorts. Character-to-character feuds should run as Collab posts, so each exchange is one original post. Pishachini's healer-witch premise needs moderation that never endorses real-world "cures". No character face may resemble a real actor or public figure. YouTube's likeness-detection tool is now open to all creators aged 18+ ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/digital/news/youtube-ai-video-labels-automatic-detection-1236758865/)), and the IT Rules cut takedown time to three hours.

Paid creators still matter, because human faces offset AI-creative fatigue. A ₹10-lakh budget buys roughly 60 nano Reels, 20 micro Reels, two mid-tier Reels and usage rights to run the top ten as ads. That is about 80 videos, a scaled-down copy of Kuku's 77-actor blitz aimed at the winning pilot. Creator content promoting Gulel should run as **Partnership ads**. Several sources say Meta treated undesignated creator-style ads as deceptive from March–May 2026, though they disagree on whether this is formal policy ([ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/meta-branded-content-rules-update/)). Creator Marketplace has been open worldwide since January 2026 ([Storika](https://www.storika.ai/guides/instagram-creator-marketplace)).

### Every Gulel frame is synthetic information under Indian law

India's IT Rules amendment covers "synthetically generated information", meaning audio or video that "appears to be real" and depicts a person in a way "likely to be perceived as indistinguishable from a natural person" ([Khaitan & Co](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2026-02/ERGO%20-%20IT%20Rules%20Amendment%20-%2016%20February%202026.pdf)). Seedance and Kling humans with ElevenLabs voices fall squarely inside that definition, and none of the good-faith exclusions (text, routine edits, accessibility) apply. The table sets out each regime as of September 2026.

| Rule | Status | Requirement | Gulel action |
|---|---|---|---|
| IT Rules amendment (G.S.R. 120(E)) | **In force 20 Feb 2026** | Synthetic content prominently labelled with permanent metadata. The practical conclusion is to label everything, everywhere, from the first post. On Instagram and YouTube the label costs no reach, while an unlabelled account loses recommendations. In the product, it can become a brand claim.

## Both ad toolchains need a week of plumbing before any scale spend

### Gulel's current tracking gives the algorithms almost nothing

The repository shows why paid spend would be wasted today. The Pixel fires only `PageView` ([FacebookPixel.tsx](/home/user/thegulel/src/components/FacebookPixel.tsx)). Every other event goes out as `fbq('trackCustom', …)` with no `eventID`, and of the eight helpers in [analytics.ts](/home/user/thegulel/src/lib/analytics.ts) only `analytics.share` is ever called ([ShareButton.tsx](/home/user/thegulel/src/components/ShareButton.tsx)). There is no `_fbp`/`_fbc`/`fbclid` handling and no UTM capture. [Analytics.tsx](/home/user/thegulel/src/components/Analytics.tsx) loads both gtag.js and a GTM container, which double-counts if GTM also carries a GA4 tag, and it has no Consent Mode default. It sends one UA-style `page_path` config that misses client-side route changes. The mobile app declares only the custom scheme `gulel`, with no `associatedDomains` or `intentFilters` ([app.json](/home/user/thegulel/mobile/app.json)), and thegulel.com serves no `/.well-known/` files. The server routes that should send conversions already exist: the Razorpay, Stripe and RevenueCat webhooks, the coin ad-reward endpoint and the OTP verification route. Anyone adding click-ID or UTM capture should note that Next.js 16 uses `proxy.ts` where the repo still has `src/middleware.ts` ([Next.js docs](/home/user/thegulel/node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/01-getting-started/16-proxy.md)).

### Meta: one verified portfolio, CAPI, then one broad Advantage+ campaign

Put everything under one business-verified Meta Business Portfolio: the brand and character Pages and Instagram accounts, two INR ad accounts (web and app), one Pixel dataset, both apps and the WhatsApp Business Account. Verify thegulel.com with a DNS TXT record ([Meta Business Help](https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/help/321167023127050)). Business verification usually takes 1–3 business days but can stretch to two weeks when names don't match ([Izoate](https://www.izoate.com/blog/how-to-verify-your-facebook-business-account-in-2026/)). Aggregated Event Measurement's manual eight-event ranking was removed in June 2025 ([Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/meta-announces-big-changes-to-website-conversion-campaigns/)). Meta Verified for Business costs **from ₹639 a month** in India ([Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/07/launching-meta-verified-subscription-plans-for-businesses-on-instagram-and-facebook-in-india/)) and is worth buying for the brand accounts as impersonation protection.

The Conversions API should deduplicate on an identical `event_id` within 48 hours. Email and phone go SHA-256 hashed, while `fbp`/`fbc`, IP address and user agent go unhashed ([adsuploader](https://adsuploader.com/blog/meta-conversions-api); [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/parameters/fbp-and-fbc)). Webhooks carry no browser cookies, so `_fbp`/`_fbc` must be stored on the user or order row at sign-up and checkout. With phone OTP as the login, the hashed `+91` number will be the strongest match key. In the app, RevenueCat's Meta integration sends purchases and renewals by CAPI even when the app is closed ([RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/integrations/attribution/meta-ads)). The Meta SDK should log only activation and registration events, so purchases are not counted twice.

Campaign structure follows from the learning-phase math. Every Sales, App and Leads campaign is now unified Advantage+, and Marketing API v25.0 blocked new legacy ASC/AAC campaigns ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/meta-deprecates-legacy-campaign-apis-for-advantage-structure/)). An ad set still needs about **50 optimisation events in seven days** to exit learning ([growwithsakib](https://growwithsakib.com/meta-ads-learning-phase/)), which implies a daily budget of about seven times the target CPA. At a ₹60 cost per registration, one ad set needs about ₹430 a day. At a ₹400 cost per purchase, it needs about ₹2,900. Gulel should therefore start one Advantage+ Sales campaign with a single broad India 18+ ad set optimising for `CompleteRegistration`, and move it to `Purchase` once purchases exceed 50 a week. It should run **8–15 conceptually distinct creatives**, since Andromeda-era delivery rewards diversity over count ([Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-andromeda-update-creative-strategy-2026)). A separate test lane of 20–30% of budget runs 3:2:2 tests (three hooks × two primary texts × two headlines) and graduates winners by Post ID ([adsmanagement.co](https://www.adsmanagement.co/blog/meta-ads-testing-methodology-the-3-2-2-creative-testing-method)). Detailed-targeting exclusions are gone, and Meta cites a 22.6% lower median cost per conversion without them ([Online Optimism](https://onlineoptimism.com/blog/detailed-targeting-exclusions-in-meta-ad-campaigns/)). Payers should be excluded through custom audiences instead.

The secondary Meta surfaces each have a narrow job. Click-to-WhatsApp ads open a **free 72-hour messaging window** for a bot that sends the Episode-1 link and a coin offer ([360dialog](https://360dialog.com/blog/72-hour-click-to-whatsapp-ad-window/)). Threads ads, global since January 2026 ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/21/meta-ads-global-threads.html)), can stay inside Advantage+ placements. Advantage+ creative's March 2026 AI dubbing reportedly covers Hindi, Bengali and Tamil ([digitalapplied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/meta-advantage-plus-march-2026-ai-dubbing-music-persona), secondary). That makes regional trailer tests cheap, provided a human reviews the auto-edits on Hindi copy and AI faces. Every trailer needs a policy-safe cut with no sexual framing ([Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/objectionable-content/adult-nudity-and-sexual-activity)) and no gore or shock imagery ([Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/objectionable-content/violent-graphic-content/)). This matters most for Pishachini's horror hooks.

Meta's reporting also changed twice in 2026. On 12 Jan the 7- and 28-day view windows left the API, and advertisers saw reported conversions fall 15–40% ([Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/meta-ads-attribution-window-removed-january-2026)). Since March, click-through counts only link clicks. Likes, saves and 5-second views moved to a one-day "engage-through" bucket ([Meta for Business](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/click-attribution)). Decisions should rest on 7-day link-click results checked weekly against Razorpay, Stripe and RevenueCat revenue.

### Google: fix GA4, then run App campaigns and Shorts-only Demand Gen

Google needs one GA4 property with web, Android and iOS streams. The funnel events should use recommended names (`sign_up`, `begin_checkout`, `purchase`, `earn_virtual_currency`, `spend_virtual_currency`) ([Google for Developers](https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/collection/ga4/reference/events)). Web purchases should go only from the payment webhook through the Measurement Protocol, which drops events older than 72 hours ([Google for Developers](https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/collection/protocol/ga4/sending-events)). All tags should run through a single `dataLayer`-driven GTM container. BigQuery daily export caps at 1M events a day ([GA Help](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9358801?hl=en)), and every episode play plus progress pings will hit that quickly, so budget for streaming export. Server-side GTM costs about US$120 a month and pays off only above about US$5K of monthly ad spend ([TrackingFixes](https://trackingfixes.com/server-side-tracking-cost/)). It can wait until combined spend passes about ₹4–5 lakh a month. Enhanced Conversions, unified into one setting since April 2026 ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16884284?hl=en)), should hash the E.164 phone number on sign-up and purchase. Consent Mode v2 is mandatory only for EEA and UK traffic ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13695607?hl=en)), but DPDP deadlines make it worth building now.

The campaign stack has four parts. **Android App campaigns (ACi)** are the install engine. They should optimise first for `sign_up` or an engaged-viewer proxy, then target CPA on `purchase`, then hybrid-monetisation target ROAS once AdMob is linked to Firebase, so that rewarded-ad revenue counts alongside coin revenue ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13799577?hl=en); [AdMob Help](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6383165?hl=en)). Google advises a daily budget of **50–100× target CPI** ([MegaDigital](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/google-app-campaigns/)), about ₹750–4,000 a day at a ₹15–40 CPI. Each ad group gets five 30-character headlines, five 90-character descriptions and up to 20 videos ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6357595?hl=en)), and links to a matching Play **custom store listing**. Play allows up to 50 such listings, targeted by ad-group ID ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16742242?hl=en)). **Demand Gen** with a Shorts-only channel setting ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15973205?hl=en)) carries the 15–45-second vertical cliffhanger cuts to web and app. It needs 15× target CPA a day, 50 conversions and four to six weeks before judgement ([Google](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/external_demand_gen_performance_guide.pdf)). GML 2026 added creator-video import to Demand Gen, which Google says lifts conversions about 20% ([WordStream](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/google-marketing-live-2026)). **Search** covers brand terms (Gulel, गुलेल, series titles), using AI Max's branded-search control ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-ads-gets-branded-search-controls-inside-ai-max/)), and Hindi and Hinglish category terms. **App campaigns for engagement** wait until the app has 50,000 installs ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9234102?hl=en)). Web to App Connect, which Google credits with a 2.8× conversion lift, requires the App Links that Gulel lacks ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16401018?hl=en)).

Three Google mechanics round this out. Firebase Remote Config should run the paywall experiments: free-episode count, signup coin bonus, rewarded-first ordering and VIP anchor. Play's Promotional Content cards, which averaged +5% active users and +4% revenue in beta ([Play Console](https://play.google.com/console/about/programs/promotionalcontent/)), should mark every premiere and festival coin sale. Policy constrains the copy. Google's **Dishonest Pricing Practices** policy, enforced since 28 Oct 2025, bans calling something "free" when payment is needed and requires trial length and renewal price to be shown ([Google Ads Policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15938375)). Advertiser verification in India is strict: Google suspended **1.7M advertiser accounts** there in 2025 ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/advertising/google-blocks-483-7-million-ads-in-india-suspends-1-7-million-advertiser-accounts-in-2025-95387.htm)). The legal entity name must match across Google Ads, Play, the App Store and the site footer.

The minimum always-on stack is small against the budget. It comprises one Meta ad set on registrations, one Android App campaign and one Demand Gen campaign, costing roughly **₹3,400–9,200 a day**, or ₹1–2.8 lakh a month. The deck's ₹15 Cr works out to about ₹4.1 lakh a day, 45–120 times that floor. **Money is not the constraint in Year 1. Signal quality, creative supply and payment conversion are.**

## The web funnel has to survive Instagram's in-app browser

Most social traffic will land inside Instagram's or Facebook's embedded browser, and that browser breaks the two things Gulel's funnel needs most. **Google Sign-In fails there with `403 disallowed_useragent`**, and no site setting fixes it ([Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/upcoming-security-changes-to-googles-oauth-20-authorization-endpoint-in-embedded-webviews/)). **Razorpay supports only cards there**, because UPI flows that open a pop-up page are blocked ([Razorpay Docs](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payment-gateway/web-integration/standard/troubleshooting-faqs/?preferred-country=IN)). On iOS no link can leave Instagram's browser automatically, and video that is unmuted without a user tap gets paused ([WebKit](https://webkit.org/blog/6784/new-video-policies-for-ios/)). The funnel should therefore look like this. The ad points to `thegulel.com/watch/{ep1}` with **no login wall**. Episode 1 autoplays muted behind a large "Tap for sound" control. A cliffhanger card leads to the Episode-2 paywall, where phone OTP is the default sign-in. Inside Meta's browser, an "Open in Chrome to pay with UPI" step comes before checkout. The app is offered only after the first purchase, so it cannot leak users before they pay.

Link-in-bio should run on Gulel's own domain, not Linktree. Linktree charges US$15 a month for a custom domain and builds its own domain authority ([u2l.ai](https://u2l.ai/blog/linktree-pricing)). A `thegulel.com/l/{account}` page per brand or character account, plus editable `/go/{code}` short links that log the click server-side and redirect, keeps every tap in Gulel's analytics and sets first-touch cookies. Universal Links and App Links need the two `.well-known` files and Expo's `associatedDomains` and auto-verified `intentFilters` ([Expo](https://expo.dev/blog/universal-and-app-links)). Firebase Dynamic Links has returned 404s since **25 Aug 2025** ([Firebase FAQ](https://firebase.google.com/support/dynamic-links-faq)). Deferred deep links can start on ChottuLink's free tier to 25K MAU ([ChottuLink](https://chottulink.com/pricing.html)) or Airbridge's free tier below 10K MAU ([Airbridge](https://www.airbridge.io/en/deeplink-plan)), plus Android's free Play Install Referrer ([GoToApp](https://gotoapp.store/blog/play-store-install-referrer)). For a login-based app, the most reliable deferred deep link is server-side state: the same phone number resumes `last_watched` wherever the user signs in. A full measurement partner (MMP) such as AppsFlyer or Adjust can wait until app-install spend is material.

Attribution should rest on UTMs that Gulel owns. Safari 26 strips `gclid` and `fbclid` but leaves UTMs intact ([Taggrs](https://taggrs.io/safari-26-tracking-changes/)). GA4 now offers only data-driven and last-click models ([Lovesdata](https://www.lovesdata.com/blog/google-analytics-attribution-models/)), and data-driven needs volume a new property lacks. On every landing, server-set cookies should record first touch (`gul_ft`, never overwritten, 180 days) and last touch (`gul_lt`). Both are copied onto the Supabase user row at sign-up and onto the purchase row, so LTV and payback can be computed in SQL by channel, character account, series and creative.

| Parameter | Convention | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `utm_source` | Where the click happened | `instagram`, `youtube`, `whatsapp`, `google`; paid Meta uses `{{site_source_name}}` |
| `utm_medium` | Matches GA4 channel groups | `social`, `paid_social`, `cpc`, `paid_video`, `messaging` (never `bio`) |
| `utm_campaign` | `{objective}_{series-slug}_{yyyymm}` | `acq_pishachini-ep1_202610` |
| `utm_content` | `{account}_{creative-id}_{hook}_{format}` | `pishachini_cr0142_h3_reel`, `gulel_bio1` |
| `utm_term` / `utm_id` | Ad set or keyword / platform campaign ID | `{{adset.name}}` / `{{campaign.id}}` |
| Custom (first-party only) | Character, series, ad, placement | `gch=`, `gsr=`, `gad={{ad.id}}`, `gpl={{placement}}` |

These parameters follow GA4's supported UTM set ([GA Help](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11242870?hl=en)) and Meta's double-brace dynamic parameters, entered at ad level ([Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/url-parameters-facebook-ads/)). Everything is lowercase, with ASCII slugs for Hindi titles.

The KPI tree runs from creative to payback. The creative layer tracks hook rate (3-second plays ÷ impressions), hold rate (ThruPlays ÷ 3-second plays) and link CTR ([Vaizle](https://insights.vaizle.com/hook-rate-hold-rate/)). The player layer tracks landing-to-play, Episode-1 completion and paywall reach. The money layer tracks sign-up, first purchase and ARPPU at D0, D7 and D30. The return layer tracks D1/D7 retention and cohort ROAS by first touch. Generic Meta benchmarks put a good hook rate at 25–30% and a scalable one at 35% ([AdManage](https://admanage.ai/blog/what-is-a-good-hook-rate-for-facebook-ads)). Short-drama UA optimises towards same-day ROAS ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/microdrama/)), and subscription apps commonly target **40–70% D30 ROAS for 12-month payback** ([Liftoff](https://liftoff.ai/blog/what-is-a-good-roas/)). No micro-drama-specific kill or scale thresholds were found. Gulel's starting rules are therefore heuristics to calibrate. Kill a creative whose hook rate is under 20% after about 5,000 impressions, or that has spent twice the target first-purchase cost without a purchase. Scale budgets 20–30% a day while 7-day-click CPA holds for three consecutive days. Refresh the bottom fifth of creatives every two weeks against the two-to-three-week fatigue curve. Incrementality testing is now affordable. Google's Conversion Lift minimum fell to **US$5,000** ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-lowers-incrementality-testing-threshold-to-5-000-for-advertisers/)), and geo lift is self-serve for Video and Demand Gen ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14097193?hl=en)). For Meta, holding out two or three comparable states for two to four weeks gives a readable test.

Retention runs on WhatsApp more than anything else, and WhatsApp now costs money. Marketing templates cost **₹0.8631 plus 18% GST** per message from 1 Jul 2026, and utility templates ₹0.115 ([MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026)). Several BSP sources report that service replies become chargeable from **1 Oct 2026**, after 1,000 free per number each month ([Wati](https://www.wati.io/en/blog/whatsapp-service-message-pricing/)). Meta caps marketing messages per user across all businesses without publishing the number ([Infobip](https://www.infobip.com/blog/what-is-whatsapp-frequency-capping)). So keep "new episode" nudges to one or two a week, and use free Channels and CTWA windows for the rest. Web push reaches iOS only for web apps added to the Home Screen ([WebKit](https://webkit.org/blog/13878/web-push-for-web-apps-on-ios-and-ipados/)). SMS needs DLT-registered templates with whitelisted URLs, `-P` headers and a 10am–9pm window ([WebEngage](https://docs.webengage.com/docs/trai-sms-dlt-regulations-india); [Kommify](https://kommify.com/blog/trai-sms-regulations/)). Re-engagement ads should target "started Episode 1, no purchase in 7 days" with Episode 2–3 cliffhangers, and "saw paywall, no purchase in 3 days" with a first-purchase offer.

The DPDP deadlines shape all of this. The Consent Manager framework goes live **13 Nov 2026** and full obligations apply from **13 May 2027**, with penalties up to ₹250 Cr ([ConsentOS](https://consentos.in/learn/dpdp-compliance-timeline/); [IDfy](https://www.idfy.com/blog/dpdp-act-faqs-2025-edition-consent-revocation-rights-notices-penalties-compliance-explained/)). Notices must itemise the data collected and its purpose, in English or any Eighth Schedule language ([Consently](https://www.consently.in/blog/dpdp-rule-3-itemized-consent-notice-guide)). Anyone under 18 is a child, needs verifiable parental consent and **may not be tracked or targeted with ads** ([MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/11/223-dpdp-rules-tracking-children-parental-consent/)). The ads and CRM Gulel builds now should already carry the controls. That means a Hindi and English consent sheet separating essential, analytics and marketing measurement, with the Pixel and Ads tags loading only after opt-in. It means an 18+ gate at sign-up, unticked separate WhatsApp and SMS opt-ins, and consent version and timestamp stored beside the attribution fields. Withdrawals must also reach Meta: opted-out users are dropped from Custom Audience uploads, and their CAPI events are suppressed.

Two of these corrections change the launch itself, not just the deck. The first is free episodes. Pratilipi's warning against early monetisation and the ₹1–2 trial norm argue for more free runway on the flagship. Gulel should test one, three and five free episodes of *Pishachini* through Remote Config. The pilots should keep Episode 1 free, and Episode 2 should be unlockable with two rewarded ads or coins, so the pilots still yield a demand signal. Whatever wins must then appear identically in the deck, the ads and the store listings. The second is VIP. It should become a ₹1–2 seven-day trial rolling into a plan priced against Kuku's ₹399 a quarter and ₹899 a year, on Razorpay UPI AutoPay rather than card-only Stripe. That is the rail on which 70–80% of the category's subscribers already pay.

## Conclusion

Gulel's structural advantage is not that AI makes episodes cheap, since every competitor now says the same. Its advantage is that episodes cost less than the ads that test them. A full season costs less than half a day of planned spend, and the entire pilot slate can be ranked for under ₹6 lakh. In a category where rivals commit ₹20–70 lakh per live-action series before seeing data ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/india-microdrama-market-studios-platforms-monetisation-126082100595_1.html)), Gulel can treat content as the experiment and paid social as the measuring instrument. Doing so turns the capital plan around. The scarce resources in Year 1 are clean conversion data, a UPI-native paywall and a supply of distinct hooks, not rupees for content. Until those exist, larger budgets mostly buy clicks from people who cannot pay inside Instagram's browser, measured by a Pixel that cannot see them buy.

The deck's reach and recovery targets become defensible only if Gulel changes what it counts and where growth comes from. "100 million viewers" should be restated as cumulative social reach, with a separate registered-viewer target in the low millions that ₹15 Cr can actually buy. The falling Year-2 and Year-3 marketing line needs a named near-zero-CAC channel, such as a telco bundle, an OEM preload or a distribution partner, because no evidence shows a paid-acquisition micro-drama app shrinking marketing while revenue grows. Mandatory AI labelling, often treated as a handicap, can be one of Gulel's few cheap advantages. Instagram and YouTube penalise unlabelled AI accounts, not labelled ones, and an openly AI-native, Hindi-original studio competing with dubbed Chinese catalogues gets a positioning claim that no live-action rival can make.
