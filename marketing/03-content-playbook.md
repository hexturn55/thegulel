```
   Reels + Shorts → "Related video"            character account)          → feed, Stories
   │                   │                           │                          │
   └───────── every cut ends on the series END CARD (Ep 2 → thegulel.com/go/{alias}) ──────┘
```

**Originality rule (Instagram Apr 2026, Facebook Jul 2025, YouTube "inauthentic content" Jul 2025):**
never post the *identical* clip on two Gulel accounts.
- The brand account posts the clip and invites the character account as a **Collab** co-author, so there's one post with shared reach.
- Character accounts post **re-voiced / re-framed** edits: new VO, POV, reaction or confession. Those count as "materially transformed".
- On YouTube, vary structure between uploads (recaps, fan-theory replies, behind-the-scenes on how the AI series is made)
  so the channel shows human authorship, not a template farm.

## 3. Hook formulas (0–3 seconds)

Every hook cut and ad opens with one of these. The hook ID (`h01`…) goes into `utm_content`.

| ID | Formula | Hindi pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| h01 | **Betrayal reveal** | "जिस पर भरोसा किया, उसी ने…" | "शादी के मंडप में दूल्हा बदल गया।" |
| h02 | **Hidden identity** | "सबने उसे ___ समझा… असल में वो ___ था।" | "सबने उसे ड्राइवर समझा… वो कंपनी का मालिक निकला।" |
| h03 | **Price of a miracle** | "हर ___ की कीमत होती है।" | "वो हर बीमारी ठीक करती है… पर कीमत क्या है?" |
| h04 | **Revenge vow** | "आज से ___ का हिसाब होगा।" | "जिसने मुझे बर्बाद किया, अब उसकी बारी है।" |
| h05 | **Forbidden choice** | "अगर तुम्हें ___ और ___ में से चुनना पड़े?" | "प्यार या परिवार — तुम क्या चुनोगे?" |
| h06 | **Cold open on action** | *(no text, 1.5 s of peak action, then title)* | Slap / scream / door burst / diya blows out |
| h07 | **Question to camera** | "क्या आप ___ करते?" | "अगर आपकी सौतेली माँ ऐसा करती, तो?" |
| h08 | **Countdown / stakes** | "सिर्फ ___ घंटे बाकी।" | "अमावस्या से पहले इलाज पूरा होना चाहिए।" |

**Rules.** Put the text on screen in the first frame (≤ 7 words, Mukta ExtraBold, inside the safe zone). Burn in Hindi captions,
since many viewers watch muted. Never open on a logo. Never show gore or sexual content in the first 3 s
(Meta and Google policy risk). Evoke, don't show.

## 4. Language

- **Captions on video:** Devanagari Hindi (Tier 2/3 and older viewers read it faster).
- **Post captions:** first line in Devanagari, second line in Roman Hinglish. Research: Devanagari wins Tier 2/3,
  Hinglish wins metros and 18–34s. Test both via Trial Reels.
- **Ads:** run the Devanagari hook as primary and a Hinglish variant as the second text option (Meta lets you add up to 5 primary texts).
- **Hashtags:** 3–5 max: one series tag, one genre tag, one Gulel tag. Examples: `#Pishachini #HorrorStory #GulelOriginal`.
  Brand tags: `#Gulel #GulelOriginal #VerticalDrama #HindiDrama #MicroDrama`.

| Account | Reels / Shorts | Feed static / carousel | Stories | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Trial Reels** (IG) | +2/day on the brand account: test hook variants on non-followers before they become ads | — | — | Winners → paid |

**Best times.** 19:00–23:30 IST (evening relax viewing, 69%), with a lunch window at 13:00–14:30.
Avoid dropping hero posts during India T20I evenings. Schedule everything in **Meta Business Suite → Planner**.

## 6. Community management (characters answer in character)

- **First 60 minutes after posting:** the character replies to the top 10 comments *in character*.
  Pishachini gives cryptic cures. वारिस gives one-line vows. The bride replies like a diary.
  The CEO is polite, with a twist.
- **Turn comments into content:** pin the best question and answer it with a QA reply Reel the next day.
- **Spoilers:** never reveal Ep 2 outcomes in comments. The answer is always "Episode 2 में देखिए… link in bio".
- **"Is this AI?"** Answer openly: "हाँ, मैं Gulel की AI किरदार हूँ — पर मेरी कहानी असली डर देती है।"
  Never deny being AI (ASCI + Instagram "AI-generated profile" rules).
- **Negative or abusive comments:** hide, don't argue. Escalate complaints about likeness to real people (YouTube likeness detection;
  3-hour takedown window under the IT Rules 2026) to the team the same day.
- **Collab dramas:** once a week, two character accounts co-post a VS post (e.g., "पिशाचिनी vs वारिस — किससे ज़्यादा डर लगता है?").
